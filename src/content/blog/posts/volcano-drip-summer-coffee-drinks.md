Summer changes what we want from coffee. Less bitterness at the rim of the cup. More refreshment. Something you can sip on the porch, at a festival, or after a morning at the lake — without giving up the character that makes single-origin worth drinking in the first place.

The good news: volcanic-soil coffees hold up beautifully in cold formats. Bright Costa Rican notes stay clean over ice. Indonesian depth turns silky in cold brew. And a few simple recipes turn your home counter into the best seat at the party.

We put together five **Volcano Drip summer coffee drinks** you can make at home — plus where each roast shines. For step-by-step versions of our house favourites, see [signature drinks](/signature-drinks). ## 1. Japanese iced pour over (flash brew)

**Best with:** [Primera Luz](/origins/costa-rica) (Costa Rica) or [Inca Ascent](/origins/peru) (Peru)

Flash brew is pour over brewed hot directly onto ice. You get the clarity of hot extraction with immediate chill — brighter and more aromatic than batch cold brew.

**Quick method**

- Weigh **20 g** coffee, ground medium-fine (see our [grind guide](/blog/coffee-grind-guide))
- Place **150 g ice** in your server or carafe
- Brew with **150 g hot water** (93–96°C) using your usual pour-over routine — bloom, then slow circles
- Serve immediately over fresh ice if needed

**Why it works in summer:** Light and medium roasts keep their citrus, stone fruit, and floral notes. Primera Luz in particular reads like sunshine in a glass.

## 2. Classic cold brew concentrate

**Best with:** [Sumatra Black](/origins/indonesia) (Indonesia) or [Antigua Ember](/origins/guatemala) (Guatemala)

Cold brew is the low-effort summer workhorse. Coarse grind, cold water, long steep — smooth, sweet, low-acid.

**Quick method**

- **1:8** ratio by weight (e.g. 100 g coffee to 800 g cold water), coarse grind
- Steep **14–18 hours** in the fridge, then strain
- Dilute concentrate **1:1** with water or milk over ice

**Serving idea:** One part concentrate, one part tonic water, orange peel. A darker, smoother cousin of our [Lava Shot](/signature-drinks).

## 3. The Lava Shot (espresso tonic)

**Best with:** [Antigua Ember](/origins/guatemala) or any medium roast you love as espresso

This is Volcano Drip's signature **espresso tonic** — effervescent, bitter-sweet, and built for patio weather.

**Quick method**

- Fill a tall glass with ice
- Add **4–6 oz** premium tonic water
- Pull a **double espresso** and pour slowly over the back of a spoon to layer
- Garnish with orange peel

**Twist:** Blood orange or pineapple syrup and a dehydrated orange slice. Full recipe on our [signature drinks](/signature-drinks) page.

## 4. The Magma Cold Brew (tropical)

**Best with:** [Sumatra Black](/origins/indonesia)

Cold brew meets tropical fruit — sweet entry, bright middle, bold coffee finish.

**Quick method**

- Fill a tall glass with ice
- Pour **pineapple juice** or **coconut water** halfway
- Layer **Volcano Drip cold brew concentrate** on top
- Garnish with fresh orange

Perfect when you want something that feels like a treat without added syrup. See the full [Magma Cold Brew](/signature-drinks) recipe for proportions and variations.

## 5. Iced latte with shaken espresso

**Best with:** [Copán Rise](/origins/honduras) (Honduras) or [Inca Ascent](/origins/peru)

Shaking espresso with ice and a splash of milk (dairy or oat) aerates the shot and gives you a frothy, café-style iced latte without an espresso machine upgrade.

**Quick method**

- Pull **1–2 shots** of espresso into a shaker with ice
- Shake **10–15 seconds** until cold and frothy
- Strain into a glass with fresh ice; top with **2–4 oz** cold milk
- Optional: **½ tsp** vanilla or cinnamon in the shaker

Copán Rise brings cocoa and stone fruit through the milk without getting lost.

## Gear and grind — the summer essentials

Summer drinks fail when grind is wrong or coffee is stale. A few reminders:

- **Pour over / flash brew:** medium-fine — not as fine as espresso, not as coarse as French press
- **Cold brew:** coarse, like sea salt
- **Espresso drinks:** dial in for your machine; start with a medium roast you know well

Use coffee within **2–4 weeks of roast** for iced methods where brightness matters. Our [freshness guide](/blog/when-to-drink-coffee-after-roasting) explains why.
