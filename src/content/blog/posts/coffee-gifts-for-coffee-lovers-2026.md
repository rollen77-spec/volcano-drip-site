## Rule #1: Check How They Brew Before You Pick a Flavour

The most common coffee gift mistake has nothing to do with taste. It's buying whole beans for someone without a grinder, or pre-ground coffee for someone who grinds fresh every morning.

So before anything else, do a little light snooping:

- **Do they own a grinder?** Go whole bean. It stays fresher for longer once the bag is open.
- **No grinder?** Pick a grind that matches their brewer. A French press wants coarse; a drip machine wants medium.
- **Not sure what they use?** Peek at their counter next time you visit, or just ask. "What's your coffee setup?" is a question coffee people love answering.

Our [Complete Coffee Grind Guide](/blog/coffee-grind-guide) breaks down every grind size and the brewer it belongs to, if you want to get it exactly right. Every Volcano Drip bag can be ordered in the grind you need.

## Why Volcanic Coffee Makes a Better Gift

Here's a fun fact to share over the wrapping paper: at 3,404m, nighttime temperatures where Sumatra Black grows can drop close to freezing, even near the equator. That stress slows the coffee cherry down, giving it more time to build sugars and flavour.

Pair that with mineral-rich volcanic soil, and you get coffee with more sweetness, more complexity and a cleaner finish. It's the reason so many of the world's most celebrated coffees grow near volcanoes. (The full story is in [Why Volcanic Soil Produces Better Coffee](/blog/why-volcanic-soil-produces-better-coffee).)

In other words, you're not just giving coffee. You're giving a little piece of a volcano. That's a much better line for the gift tag.
